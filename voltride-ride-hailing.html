01 — Executive Summary

A Moment You Can’t Miss

Indonesia’s electric ride-hailing market is at a turning point. Public trust in Green SM was shaken after the April 2026 incident. This is a rare opportunity to build a strong local brand.

📍 Market Context

Green SM only entered Indonesia in December 2024 and is still struggling with a trust crisis after the Bekasi incident. Indonesian netizens are boycotting, comments are closed, and the X account is privatized. A substantial opening exists for a new, trusted local player.

🎯 VoltRide Opportunity

VoltRide delivers electric ride-hailing service fully rooted in Indonesia — local operators, local fleet, local empathy. With this momentum, we are ready to become the country’s most loved EV mobility brand.

🚘 Fleet & Technology

We use locally manufactured and ASEAN electric vehicles certified by SNI, backed by an internationally standard app platform, multimodal payment systems, and a real-time safety dashboard for drivers and passengers.

🤝 Partnership Model

VoltRide is open to strategic partnership schemes: co-branding, joint venture operations, B2B corporate fleets, and white-label platforms for regional partners. Flexible, structured, and mutually beneficial.

02 — Market Problems

Where Competitors Failed

Every gap left by Green SM is an opportunity for a player who truly understands the needs of Indonesian passengers.

😶

Careless Crisis Response

After the fatal Bekasi incident, Green SM did not include an apology in its official statement. Indonesian passengers do not want service from a brand that does not prioritize their safety.

🌍

Foreign Brand, Foreign Management

Controlled from Vietnam by foreign investors, Green SM still fails to grasp the local Indonesian cultural nuances — from public communication to service standards.

🚦

Operational Safety Concerns

The Ministry of Transportation summoned Green SM and threatened to revoke its operating license. Passengers are losing confidence in the fleet and driver safety standards.

📵

Closing Off Public Feedback

Closing Instagram comments and privatizing the X account made the image worse. Indonesian netizens responded with a massive, viral boycott movement.

🏙️

Limited Geographic Coverage

Green SM remains concentrated in major cities. Hundreds of tier-2 and tier-3 cities across Indonesia still lack quality EV mobility service.

💳

Weak Local Payment Ecosystem

It is not fully integrated with popular local wallets such as GoPay, OVO, Dana, and LinkAja — payment methods used by the majority of Indonesian passengers.
